How do I learn what I wrote in a logbook?

Write a cell: what you want to remember in the right column, a cue or the answer in the left. Touch and hold the cell, let go, and tap Learn this cell. It joins your reviews as a card, the right column in front and the left on the back. A cell with nothing on the left is shown as it is and graded; a cell with nothing on the right doesn’t become a card.

To learn the whole book, tap ⋯ at the top of the logbook and turn on Learn every cell: every cell with writing in both columns, now and from then on, joins your reviews. To stop learning one, hold it and tap Stop learning this cell.

Words you hide with the highlighter (Cover) are for quizzing yourself in the book. They don’t go into review.

What does Say it need? Is my voice kept?

Touch and hold the empty cell at the end of a page and speak; let go and it’s a new cell. Slide your finger to the other column while you talk, and the rest goes there. You can also add Say it as a Home Screen widget, and on iOS 18 or later to Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button.

The first time, iOS asks for the microphone and speech recognition. What you say is turned into text on your device, and the sound of your voice isn’t kept. Pick the language in Settings › Logbook › Language for Say it. The first time you use a language, iOS may need to download it, so be online.

How is the next review decided?

After you turn a card over, tap Forgot, Fuzzy or Got it. A card you forgot or were fuzzy on comes back a few more times in the same round, and its button says how many more times you need to get it. Under Got it you see when the card comes back; the gap is worked out by the memory model from your own review history.

The first time you open the app each study day, it sets today’s batch: the cards that are due, up to Settings › Review › Most Reviews a Day. What doesn’t fit moves to a day with room. Review Intensity is Light, Standard or Thorough; the more thorough, the closer together the reviews.

To move one card yourself, touch and hold it in its deck and choose Reschedule.

I’m not getting review reminders

The first time you finish a round, the app asks what time to remind you each day; iOS asks for notifications only after you tap OK. After that, go to Settings › Reminders, turn on Notifications, then Morning Reminder or Evening Reminder and their times. Also allow notifications for Memo Clock in the system Settings under Notifications.

How do I import from Anki?

  1. In Anki on a computer, export the deck as “Anki Deck Package (.apkg)” and tick “Support older Anki versions”.
  2. Save the .apkg file to the Files app on your iPad or iPhone, by AirDrop or iCloud Drive.
  3. In Memo Clock, tap ＋ and choose Import Anki Deck or Shared File (on iPhone, Import File), then pick the file.

For cards you already studied, you choose Keep Their Progress or Start Over as New Cards; for new cards, you’re asked over how many days to learn them. Text, formulas, images and cloze cards come across; sounds and text formatting (colors, bold) don’t. Cards you already have are skipped.

How do I photograph a problem and hide the answer?

Tap ＋ and choose Take Photo or Scan. Select the text you need on the photo and put it in the answer; where there’s no text to select, tap Frame it instead and frame part of the picture.

Then tap Cover and tap a word, or swipe across part of the answer, to hide it. Tap Done and save. In review, each cover is a yellow bar; tap it to reveal what’s underneath. The same works for a card you type with New Card. To keep the photo in a logbook instead, tap Add to and the logbook at the bottom.

What do I need for handwriting? Can I search it?

Write on an iPad with Apple Pencil: tap ＋ and choose Notebook, in A4, Letter, 16:9 and other sizes. PDF, Word and PowerPoint files you import can be written on too.

Chinese and English in your handwriting and photos are recognized on your device, and then the search field on the Desk finds them (⌘F with a keyboard), one line per result, with the sentence it found and where it is. Give it a moment after writing; very messy writing may not be recognized, but your strokes are never changed.

Why does writing formulas by hand need a download?

The formula keys (fractions, roots, sub- and superscripts, integrals, matrices, chemistry and more) need nothing extra. Writing a formula by hand and having it typeset uses a recognition model of about 185 MB. You’re asked the first time you tap Write, and nothing is downloaded until you tap Download; after that it works on your device.

It needs iOS 18 or later. To remove it, go to Settings › New Card › Handwritten Formulas.

I got a new iPad or iPhone. How does my stuff get there?

Sign in to the same Apple ID on the new device with iCloud turned on, then install and open Memo Clock. Your decks and review history, logbooks, notebooks and PDFs, word books and their progress, your Today widgets and your streak come over through your own iCloud by themselves; with a lot of content this takes a few minutes.

Focus time isn’t part of the automatic sync: on the old device go to Settings › iCloud Sync and tap Upload to iCloud, then on the new device tap Sync to This Device. It merges card by card and doesn’t delete anything already on the new device.

Where you placed things on the Desk is kept on each device and doesn’t carry over.

My two devices show different things

  • Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on.
  • Open Settings › iCloud Sync and look at the This Device row. “Syncing automatically” means it is syncing, and a change usually reaches the other device within a few minutes; to hurry it along, tap Send This Device's Changes.
  • “On this device only” means this device isn’t connected to automatic sync right now: tap Upload to iCloud, then tap Sync to This Device on the other one.

Still not right? Email us a screenshot of the Sync Log & Diagnostics page.

How do I back up, share or delete my data?

Your content is kept in your own iCloud. On top of that, Settings › iCloud Sync › Backup & Restore saves everything to a single .memoclockbackup file. Auto Backup does this every week unless you choose every day or off; the backups go to the Memo Clock folder in iCloud Drive in the Files app (on the device if there’s no iCloud), and the latest four are kept. Restoring merges and never deletes what you have, and you can undo it.

To give a deck to a classmate, open it, tap ⋯ › Share this deck and choose PDF, Spreadsheet (CSV), Anki Deck or Memo Clock File. A notebook or a logbook can go with Share this book; your classmate sees a preview first, then puts it on their own Desk.

To delete one thing, touch and hold it on the Desk and choose Delete; it’s deleted on your other devices too. It stays in Recently Deleted (under ··· at the top of the Desk) for 30 days, and you can put it back. To remove everything, delete Memo Clock from each device, then in the system Settings tap your name, go to iCloud storage management, find Memo Clock and delete its data, and delete the Memo Clock folder in iCloud Drive.

What exactly is stored and where is described in the Privacy Policy.

The app says “Storage unavailable”

The app pauses so that nothing you add gets lost. Quit Memo Clock completely (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again. If the screen comes back, tap Copy diagnostics on it and paste the text into an email to us.

Is there anything else to pay for?

No. Buy once and every feature is yours: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, and no account to sign up for. It downloads on both iPad and iPhone with the same Apple ID and needs iPadOS 17 / iOS 17 or later; writing formulas by hand needs iOS 18.
