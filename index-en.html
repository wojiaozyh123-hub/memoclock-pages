Classical conditioning

What is classical conditioning? Give an example.

A neutral stimulus, paired again and again with one that already triggers a response, comes to trigger that response on its own.

NSneutral stimulus: the bell USunconditioned stimulus: food CRconditioned response: drooling at the bell

Pavlov’s dogs: after the bell rang with food many times, the bell alone made them drool.

ECON outline
Write it down
A logbook with one note per cell: the content on the right, a cue on the left. Hold the last cell of a page and say it; snap a slide or the whiteboard and drop the text in. On iPad, take notes with Apple Pencil, and open PDF, Word and PowerPoint files to read and write on.
Turn it into review
Hold a cell and choose Learn this cell: it becomes a card, the right column in front and the left on the back. Or learn every cell in the book. Circle any part of a notebook page or a PDF to review it.
Review on time
Each day starts with today’s batch, up to the number you choose. Grade Forgot, Fuzzy or Got it, and every button tells you when the card comes back. Pick your reminder time, and keep your streak with make-ups.
Words for English
IELTS, CET-4, CET-6 and Academic English in three levels come with the app, with meanings and examples. Paste your own word list to make a book.
Formulas
Formula keys for fractions, roots, integrals, matrices and chemistry. On iOS 18, write a formula by hand and it is typeset for you.
Your desk and your day
Decks, logbooks, notebooks and word books on one desk. Build your Today page from widgets: Schedule with your Calendar and Reminders, your class timetable, a Pomodoro timer, white noise.
Your data stays yours
Syncs your iPad and iPhone through your own iCloud, and backs everything up to a file every week. Imports Anki decks with their progress. No account, no server, no ads.
